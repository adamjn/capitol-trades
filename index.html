What is the STOCK Act?

The Stop Trading on Congressional Knowledge (STOCK) Act was signed into law in 2012. It requires members of Congress, senior congressional staff, and certain executive branch officials to publicly disclose their stock trades within 45 days of the transaction.

This website tracks these disclosures to promote transparency and help citizens understand how their representatives are investing.
